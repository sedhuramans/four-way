# 🎯 Render Dashboard - Exact Steps to Find Resume Button

## Step-by-Step Visual Guide

### Step 1: Login to Render
1. Go to: **https://dashboard.render.com**
2. **Login** with your credentials

### Step 2: Find Your Service
1. You'll see the **Dashboard** with all your services
2. Look for: **"fourways-international-trading"** (your service name)
3. It will show status: **"Suspended"** or **"Sleeping"**

### Step 3: Click on Your Service
1. **Click on the service name**: "fourways-international-trading"
2. This opens the **service details page**

### Step 4: Look for Resume Button
The Resume/Restart button location depends on the suspension type:

#### Option A: Top of Service Page
- Look at the **top banner** (usually red/orange)
- Text might say: *"Service is suspended"*
- Button will say: **"Resume Service"** or **"Restart"**

#### Option B: Settings Tab
1. Click **"Settings"** tab (top navigation)
2. Scroll down to **"Service Status"** section
3. Look for **"Resume"** or **"Restart"** button

#### Option C: Overview Tab
1. Stay on **"Overview"** tab (default)
2. Look for status banner with **"Resume"** button
3. Or check the **"Actions"** section on the right

### Step 5: Click Resume
1. **Click the Resume/Restart button**
2. **Confirm** if prompted
3. **Wait 2-3 minutes** for service to start

## 🔍 What to Look For

### Service Status Indicators:
- ❌ **"Suspended"** - Service is offline
- 🟡 **"Starting"** - Service is waking up
- ✅ **"Live"** - Service is running

### Button Text Variations:
- **"Resume Service"**
- **"Restart Service"**
- **"Wake Up Service"**
- **"Start Service"**

## 📱 Mobile Dashboard
If using mobile:
1. **Tap** on your service name
2. **Scroll down** to find status section
3. **Tap Resume** button

## 🆘 If You Can't Find the Button

### Alternative Method 1: Redeploy
1. Go to **"Deploys"** tab
2. Click **"Deploy latest commit"**
3. This will also wake up the service

### Alternative Method 2: Manual Deploy
1. In **"Settings"** tab
2. Find **"Manual Deploy"** section
3. Click **"Deploy"** button

## 🎯 Expected Result
After clicking Resume:
- Status changes to **"Starting"**
- Then to **"Live"** (2-3 minutes)
- Your website becomes accessible again

## 📞 Still Can't Find It?
The button is definitely there! Look for:
- Any **orange/red banner** at the top
- **Status section** in Settings
- **Actions panel** on the right side
- **Service controls** near the service name

The exact location varies slightly based on Render's UI updates, but it's always prominently displayed when a service is suspended.
